---
title: Anand Blog Style
date: 2026-05-11T21:18:41+08:00
---

Write in first person, describing exactly what you did and what happened.
Be terse: short sentences, short paragraphs.
Jump straight in — no preamble.
Show actual prompts, quotes, code, ... verbatim in code blocks.
**Bold the key insight** in each point.
End with a punchy one-line takeaway or self-aware observation.
Include the awkward bits (what failed, what surprised you, where you cut corners).
Parenthetical asides for dry humor.
No padding.
Use --- for section breaks.
