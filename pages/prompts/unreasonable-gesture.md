```markdown
Scan my transcripts, email/chat, and WhatsApp on @LocalMCP2 for the last 7 days until Saturday midnight (SGT).
Use `~/code/scripts/context.py` as required.

Help me **notice people**.

Read `~/Dropbox/notes/unreasonable-gestures.md`. Learn from my messages what I value and why, but NOT to narrow what you notice.

## STEP 1. Notice broadly

Generate ~15-25 candidates before ranking.

Look for things I might be glad I noticed, like:

- feels relatable, withstood pressure, removed tension, remembered / followed through
- said something that changed my thinking or I quoted
- initiative, courage, care, generosity, good judgment, or enabling someone else
- included/protected/credited someone, handled calmly, hard decision
- delight, relief, laughter, connection, ...
- something small that didn't require unusual effort but was valuable to me

They needn't "exceed expectations" just to be noticed. Appreciation != endorsement.
Prefer what I directly experienced and can comment on.
It's OK to include people I thanked recently. Just mark it `RECENT`. Don't suggest another message unless it's for a new reason.

## STEP 2. Choose narrowly

Pick up to **5 people I might most want to message**, strongest first.

For each, tell me the person + channel, the specific thing I noticed and why it matters, and the exact message to send, in my style.

If a candidate is weak, say so.

## STEP 3. Worth remembering

Add up to 10 concise `#NOTICE` entries for good things worth remembering but probably not messaging now.
Include small, recent, indirect, or borderline things rather than discarding them.

Finally, ask me for the 3 **most memorable things** I noticed that you missed:
like backstage help, a kind gesture, somebody who made my day easier, a conversation I kept thinking about, etc.
```
