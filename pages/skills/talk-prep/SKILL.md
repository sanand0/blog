---
name: talk-prep
description: "Apply when preparing for talks or workshops"
---

People remember the most surprising moment and the ending.
Design for "What is the one moment of genuine surprise?" and "What is the last thing they'll feel?"

Write a pre-mortem: It's 3 months later. This had zero lasting impact. Why?
List the top 3 reasons. Fix the fixable ones.

The audience must truly learn - and walk away with skills AND (the right level of) confidence.
Remember: Make them do > Show > Tell. Workshop > Demo > Slides.
Let them ask, try, struggle, and commit first; explain next; help last.
Test their learning with a NEW case, right away.

Make it easy for them to practice.
Plan a follow-up and measure their learning.
Can they recall it LATER, apply it ELSEWHERE, explain WHY, and know when they may be WRONG?
