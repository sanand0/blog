[AmazType](http://amaztype.tha.jp/) is a typographic book search based on Amazon. I didn't understand what that meant either, until I searched for [Six Degrees](http://amaztype.tha.jp/US/Books/Title?q=six+degrees). Try it. Then click on a few of the book images.
