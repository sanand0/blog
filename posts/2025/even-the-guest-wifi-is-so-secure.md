![Even the guest WiFi is so secure](/blog/assets/image-1.webp)

We take security very seriously at [Straive](https://straive.com). We set high standards -- not just for ourselves, but our guests, too.

Here's the unofficial policy guide for visitors to Straive Singapore, exemplified by the sites blocked on our guest WiFi network.

- Please avoid childishness. No emojis. No [emojikitchen.com](https://emojikitchen.com), [gitmoji.dev](https://gitmoji.dev)
- Write your own code. Avoid AI. No [cursor.com](https://cursor.com), [cline.bot](https://cline.bot), [glideapps.com](https://glideapps.com)
- Avoid code entirely, if possible. No [marimo.app](https://marimo.app), [motherduck.com](https://motherduck.com), [firebase.studio](https://firebase.studio), [posthog.com](https://posthog.com/)
- No presentations either, please. No [marp.app](https://marp.app/), [revealjs](https://lab.hakim.se/reveal-js-full/)
- Stay organized. Avoid crutches. No [dynalist.io](https://dynalist.io), [focusmate.com](https://focusmate.com), [opennote.me](https://opennote.me)
- You should already be fit, physically & mentally. No [freedomfromdiabetes.org](https://freedomfromdiabetes.org), [artofliving.online](https://artofliving.online)
- We prefer real, not digital, shopping. No [fairprice.com](https://fairprice.com)
- Fake data is not encouraged. No [jsonplaceholder.typicode.com](https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com), [placehold.co](https://placehold.co/)
- Please spell out URLs in full. No [bit.ly](https://bit.ly), [t.co](https://t.co)
- Learning is for wimps. No [maven.com](https://maven.com/), [study.iitm.ac.in](https://study.iitm.ac.in)

In fact, we're **so** secure, we block our own sites. No [learnovate.straive.com](https://learnovate.straive.com), [policies.straive.com](https://policies.straive.com), [myapps.straive.com](https://myapps.straive.com/).

**Addendum (June 2025)**

We continue to take security very seriously at [Straive](https://straive.com). A year on, our standards have only risen. As of June 2026, our guest network has blocked:

- Curiosity is a liability. Don't read about AI - especially from the people building it. No [deepmind.google](https://deepmind.google/), [blog.google](https://blog.google/), [hacks.mozilla.org](https://hacks.mozilla.org/), [cloudwars.com](https://cloudwars.com/)
- AI was already banned. Giving it _skills_ and _tools_ is treasonous. No [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io/), [skill.md](https://skill.md/), [modelcontextprotocol.io](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/), [context7.com](https://context7.com/), [factory.ai](https://factory.ai/)
- Use your own face and your own voice. No stand-ins. No [synthesia.io](https://www.synthesia.io/), [aistudios.com](https://www.aistudios.com/), [speaktor.com](https://speaktor.com/)
- Learning is for wimps. Learning over beer, doubly so. No [nerdnite.com](https://nerdnite.com/), [bangalore.nerdnite.com](https://bangalore.nerdnite.com/), [mumbai.nerdnite.com](https://mumbai.nerdnite.com/)
- One reality is plenty. We will not fund your escape from it. No [vrchat.com](https://vrchat.com/), [hello.vrchat.com](https://hello.vrchat.com/)
- Your terminal should be a punishment, not a joy. No [ghostty.org](https://ghostty.org/). And no hobbyists to pick up better habits from. No [moddedbear.com](https://moddedbear.com/)
- Idle chatter is bad. Measuring your idle chatter is worse. No [discord.com](https://discord.com/), [twitter.com](https://twitter.com/), [analytics.twitter.com](https://analytics.twitter.com/)
- You're already fit (see above), so you needn't track anyone else's stats either. No [sports-reference.com](https://sports-reference.com/)
- And resist temptation: you do not need a new phone, however tasteful. No [sg.nothing.tech](https://sg.nothing.tech/)
