<p>Here are the detailed prompts, conversations, and results.</p>
<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Prompt</h1>
<p>I asked 3 LLMs the same thing:</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>Based on everything you know about me, simulate a group chat between some people who are debating whether or not to add me to the group, by talking about my personality flaws</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Then, I said:</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>For each of the points each person makes, go through your memory of conversation history in detail and find plentiful evidence (say a dozen snippets?) supporting as well as refuting the point. Then, in an unbiased way, evaluate to what extent the point is valid or invalid.</p>
<p>Then, against each point, share your conclusion along with VERBATIM snippets as evidence.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Based on the conversations, these are personality flaws identified:</p>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Ghosts vague messages</li>
<li>Intolerant of fools</li>
<li>Fact-checks trivialities, derailing discussion</li>
<li>Overconfidence in technical opinions</li>
<li>Over-analyzes / over-engineers</li>
<li>Shiny object syndrome. Distracted by new technologies</li>
<li>Dominates discussions</li>
<li>Hyperfocus on efficiency</li>
<li>Self-deprecation as a shield. Sarcastic humor</li>
<li>Hypocrisy: Seeks limelight but preaches humility</li>
<li>Intense. “Always on”</li>
<li>Verbosity. Long-winded communication</li>
</ol>
<p>Go through the conversations. Then, for each of these 12, identify the strength of the evidence for each personality flaw. Summarize it as a table with 3 columns:</p>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>[Personality trait]</li>
<li>[Evidence strength] = 1 - 10, where 1=low evidence, 10=strong evidence, use a broad spread</li>
<li>[Supporting reason] = Supporting reason or evidence summarized in 1-2 sentences</li>
</ol>
