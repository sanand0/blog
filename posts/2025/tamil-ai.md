I was testing LLMs' sense of Tamil humor with this quote:

> Extend this post with more funny Tamil words that end with .ai - mentioning why they're funny.
>
> Chenn.ai is the artificial intelligence capital of India.\
> Kadal.ai\
> Kad.ai\
> Dos.ai\
> Vad.ai\
> Ad.ai\
> Thal.ai\
> Mallig.ai\
> Aratt.ai\
> And finally\
> Podad.ai\
> All spoken in namma bash.ai 😅

The Chinese models didn't fare well.

[**DeepSeek**](https://chat.deepseek.com/share/6gswk62r2g6bs19qqs) made up words.

- Mood.ai - An AI that perfectly captures your mood.
- Sokk.ai - The AI for when you're bored.
- Thanni.ai - A hydration assistant.

[**Qwen**](https://chat.qwen.ai/s/cdccbdfd-691d-40d1-ac0e-ed87375fa5bd?fev=0.0.222https://chat.qwen.ai/s/cdccbdfd-691d-40d1-ac0e-ed87375fa5bd?fev=0.0.222) too.

- Kozhamb.ai - AI cooking spicy emotional support curry.
- Kuttr.ai - it's got drama and a side of gossip.
- Sutta.ai - The AI that's seen things too many things.

[**Kimi**](http://Click the link to view conversation with Kimi AI Assistant https://www.kimi.com/share/d3jipjcn907c4b502agg) was confused with Hindi.

- Kalyan.ai - The match-making AI.
- Gol.ai - rounds up to the nearest "gol" (ten).

[**Claude**](https://claude.ai/chat/6b57a7f9-18d7-4193-92c7-629f2a9f54a1) didn't know Tamil well either.

- Kumb.ai - Worships AI models every 12 years.
- Thamb.ai - Younger sibling AI.
- Chet.ai - The AI assistant named after the city.

[**ChatGPT**](https://chatgpt.com/share/68e72ceb-d5bc-800c-87d5-d350f68db06d) got every word right, but has no sense of humor.

- Sir.ai - "Prison." Where you go after 15 failed CAPTCHA attempts.
- Il.ai - "Leaf." For all your "green AI" posts. 🌿
- Vil.ai - "Price." Where your token bill lives. 💸

[**Grok**](https://x.com/i/grok/share/kvTspc11OoECJKu7hLckhlNdX) was a bit better. Got the words right and some were funny.

- Mand.ai - skull-crushing smarts.
- Maapill.ai - the ultimate matchmaker.
- Yaan.ai - elephant-powered AI? Trunk calls incoming!

The app that really got this right was [**Gemini**](https://g.co/gemini/share/53206ab44a8f).

- Tholl.ai (தொல்லை - Nuisance) - annoyance as a service.
- Kaval.ai (கவலை - Worry) - AI that simulates anxiety, so you don't have to.
- Vazhukk.ai (வழுக்கை - Bald Head) - AI satellite imagery to track receding hairlines.
- Aam.ai (ஆமை - Tortoise) - slow AI.
- Sand.ai (சண்டை - Fight) - Discourse-as-a-Disaster service.
- Sodhan.ai (சோதனை - Ordeal) - AI that exists to test your patience.
