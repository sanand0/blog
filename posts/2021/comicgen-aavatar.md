https://gramener.com/comicgen/v1/

The variations are staggering. It throws up surprising ones too, like crossdressers or bald women in sarees 🙂

- Who needs this?
- What could they do with it?

I'm not sure. If you have ideas or know someone who might, please let me know 🙏
