Some ideas on what we can do less, thanks to AI.

Why? Because [less is more](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/less-is-more/) and we keep getting advice on what to do _more_ of, not what to do _less_ of.

1. **Skip meetings you don't speak in**. Agents can summarize the transcript.
2. **Stop taking notes**. Agents can summarize a recording.
3. **Stop reading group chats**. Agents can summarize [WhatsApp groups](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/generative-ai-whatsapp-group-podcast/), [Hacker News](https://github.com/sanand0/blog/blob/7f6d9b2dc0ec3a2303778b045434d672c36e154c/pages/prompts/fragments.md#hacker-news-thread-summary), [Twitter](https://github.com/sanand0/blog/blob/7f6d9b2dc0ec3a2303778b045434d672c36e154c/pages/prompts/fragments.md#twitter-thread-summary), and more.
4. **Skip translated versions**. Agents translate _on the fly_. For you, _and_ your audience.
5. **Stop weekly status reports**. Agents can tell you (and your manager and team) the status.
6. **Stop building dashboards**. Agents can directly answer "what's wrong" or "what's next" directly.
7. **Stop building software at first**. Tell the agent to do the work. Let it write software if needed. Save it if you use it often.
8. **Stop writing requirements**. Agents can prototype. Test if that's what you need, first.
9. **Stop teaching coding**. Agents can code. Teach people to use agents.
10. **Stop traveling unless a handshake matters**. Actually, this was true even with Zoom, etc.
11. **Stop live lectures unless there's interaction**. Actually, this was true even with YouTube.
