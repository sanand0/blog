I don’t post all my writing on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sanand0/recent-activity/all/). For example:

- Fewer **strategy posts**, e.g. “[Where Enterprise AI is Headed](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/where-enterprise-ai-is-headed/)”, “[How My Innovation Team Works](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/how-the-innovation-team-works)”, etc. aren't on LinkedIn.
- Fewer **developer posts**, e.g. my [AGENTS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sanand0/scripts/refs/heads/main/agents/AGENTS.md), my [SKILL.md files](https://github.com/sanand0/scripts/tree/main/agents) files, [CLI tools](https://github.com/sanand0/scripts/), [evals](https://sanand0.github.io/datastories/tds-2026-01-p1/), etc. aren't on LinkedIn.

I also shorten content because of LinkedIn constraints. For example:

- No **links**, e.g. the list of all my AI-in-education resources
- Short **content**, e.g. my full [advice for teams using AI](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/ai-advice-for-teams/) is much longer than the LinkedIn post.
- Trimmed **prompts**, e.g. how to [convert meeting transcripts](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/transcript-ai-ded-interviews/) into a personalized org-consulting report
- Snipped **chats**, e.g. the full moves of [GPT-5.5 playing chess](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/chatgpt-is-about-fide-1600/)

I filter the LinkedIn posts, sharing what’s most useful for most people.

But some people asked if I can share the full content over email.

So, I’ve set up my **full blog**, **by email** (free, via Google Groups): https://groups.google.com/g/s-anand

Preview before you subscribe. Switch to weekly digest if it’s too much.
