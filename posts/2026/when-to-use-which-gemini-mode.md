I continue to be impressed by Gemini 3 and it's become my default agent. It writes in simpler language than ChatGPT (almost as eloquent as Claude), has much larger limits, and, of course, is [unbeaten at generating images](https://www.s-anand.net/blog/gemini-copies-images-almost-perfectly/).

The Gemini app has 3 modes: Fast, Thinking, and Pro. Here's when to use each:

1. **Simple task**, e.g., grammar check, translate, summarize, or basic question? Use **Fast**. Pro overthinks.
2. **Multi-step logic**, e.g., planning a trip with constraints, checking 15 emails, or identifying a subtle error in code? Use **Thinking**. Flash-based thinking beats Pro.
3. **Large input**, e.g. 300-page PDF, 2 hours of video, etc.? Use **Pro**. It uses the 1M+ token window well.
4. **Complex problem**, e.g. PhD-level science or a legal contract review, with high stakes? Use **Pro**.

If you hit your Pro limit (which is pretty high!), just switch to **Thinking**, which is smart enough for most jobs anyway.
