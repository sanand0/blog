X has [lists](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists). You can follow posts by any list of people - and share the list.

[LinkedIn has this feature too](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-take-advantage-linkedins-secret-lists-feature-mickey-mellen-munfe). It's tougher to use, but easy to share.

1. [Visit the posts search page](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/content/?origin=FACETED_SEARCH&sortBy=%5B%22date_posted%22%5D)
2. Select the "From member" filter and add members.
3. Bookmark or share the link.
