The [Electronic Commerce Resource Center](http://www.becrc.org/index.html) is the Yahoo of e-commerce. But if you want articles, go to [Digital Commerce Center](http://www.ec2.edu/dccenter/index.html) or [internet.com](http://ecommerce.internet.com/).
