title: Where you host determines your page rank on Google
date: "2006-09-24T12:00:00Z"
categories:

wp_id: 203
description: Hosting location significantly impacts search visibility because Google prioritizes sites hosted in the same geography as the searcher. Placing your server in a remote region can negatively affect your ranking for global audiences.
keywords: [seo, google search, server location, geolocation, web hosting, page rank]
---

[Google's dirty little secret](http://www.netlash.com/log/googles_dirty_little_secret) talks about how Google determines whether your site appears on top for a search depending on whether you and the searcher are in the same geography or not. So if your server is in Kazakhstan, expect fewer hits.
