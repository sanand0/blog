[The Mathematical Structure of Terrorism](http://www.physorg.com/news67524254.html). The frequency distribution of terrorist attacks -- be it in Iraq, Columbia, Afghanistan or anywhere in the world -- is the same. The frequency distribution of the size of terrorist groups is the same as well.
