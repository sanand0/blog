Though [Google Reader learns to share](http://googleblog.blogspot.com/2006/03/google-reader-learns-to-share.html), I haven't found it easy to see what others are reading. Even a [Google search for shared lists](http://www.google.com/search?q=-xyzzyx+site%3Awww.google.com%2Freader%2Fview) reveals very few.
