The California power crisis is [not due to computers](http://www.wired.com/news/technology/0,1282,41623,00.html). So distributed projects like [Seti@home](http://setiathome.ssl.berkeley.edu/) and [RC5](http://www.distributed.net/rc5/) are [not to blame](http://www.wired.com/news/technology/0,1282,41606,00.html).
