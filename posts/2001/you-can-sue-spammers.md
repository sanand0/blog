[Ellen won a spam case](http://www.spertus.com/ellen/Kozmo/kozmo.html) against Kozmo. If you get unsolicited e-mail from a company, you can sue them. Unless they have [an opt-out mechanism](http://news.cnet.com/news/0-1005-200-5668645.html).
