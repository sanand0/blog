I have an over-developed **dorsal striatum**. That's the brain part that makes [you want to eat when you're not hungry](http://www.salon.com/tech/feature/2002/09/30/ad_brain/index.html), just because the food's there. [via Plastic](http://www.plastic.com/article.html?sid=02/09/30/20595535)
