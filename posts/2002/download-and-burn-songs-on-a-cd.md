The music industry has taken a step away from the ridiculous. Now, on pressplay.com, you can [download and burn songs on a CD](http://www.nandotimes.com/entertainment/story/485707p-3876886c.html) for a fee. (The big deal is that, earlier, you could only stream and listen. For a fee.)
