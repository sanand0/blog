While I thought that every cell in the human body had identical genetic make-up, it turns out that [brain cells could have gained or lost some chromosomes](http://www.nature.com/nsu/021216/021216-2.html). Now, that's a pretty big discovery. [via missing matter](http://missingmatter.net/article.pl?sid=02/12/18/1611241)
