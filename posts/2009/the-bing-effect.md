This graph is the number of referrals Microsoft’s new search engine, [Bing](http://www.bing.com/), sent to my site over the last few days. Looks like the hype is dying out. Though [Bing did leapfrog Yahoo](http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/06/05/did-bing-just-leapfrog-yahoo-search/) briefly, that [lasted just one day](http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/06/07/quick-peak-bings-reign-as-2-search-engine-lasted-one-day/).
